Study tips that actually work

Guess before you run

Predicting the output - even wrongly - makes the right answer stick far better than just reading it.

Little and often

30 minutes a day beats 4 hours on Sunday. Keep your streak alive 🔥.

Struggle a little

Try for 10-20 minutes before opening the solution. Use the hint first.

Explain it out loud

If you can explain a solution to a friend (or a rubber duck), you really understand it.

Come back later

Redo an exercise from last week without looking. That's the best exam rehearsal there is.

Stuck on a word?

The glossary explains every technical term in plain English, with a tiny example. Heard "polymorphism" or "LEFT JOIN" in a meeting and went blank? Look it up in 10 seconds.
