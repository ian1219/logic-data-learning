For mentors and team leads

  • Run one lesson per session: the sections are your lecture outline and the live examples are your demo.
  • Ask learners to share a screenshot of their progress on the home page.
  • The interview questions make good discussion prompts - ask before revealing the answer.
  • Pair learners: one types, one explains the logic out loud. Swap every exercise.
